Emerging Artists 2026

Growth

Generative Art, 2026

Growth is a generative visual system vaguely inspired by early digital rave aesthetics as well as recent reflections on modern streaming culture and media consumption. The work produces constantly evolving crystalline forms. It is a rather personal work about the desire for friction and how passive consumption reduces the meaning of media.
